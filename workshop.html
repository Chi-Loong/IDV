Workshop Agenda

The workshop is a 1.5 day immersion course on data visualization.

The first two workshops are foundational and build upon the research in the area. The last day is application and practice.

Starting intro deck for the workshop.

Learning Objectives

  • Learn how to breakdown a visualization to its 3 core components: data, task, idiom.
  • Understand how data scales and ordering, if badly chosen, often leads to bad visualizations.
  • Learn why and how to frame visualization hypotheses.
  • Understand the science of perception and psychology of charting.
  • Learn about how to validation and critique visualizations.
